**Yemek kültürü cümle kalıpları:** *Turkish cuisine is famous for its kebabs and desserts.* / *In Japan, people eat rice with chopsticks.* / *Italians prefer pasta with tomato sauce; Mexicans like spicy food.* / *I'd rather have grilled fish than fried chicken because it is healthier.* / *Mantı is a traditional Turkish dish; it is boiled and served with yoghurt and garlic.*

### Metinlerde geçen dil bilgisi (anlamak için)

| Yapı | Örnek | Anlam |
|---|---|---|
| will / won't (tahmin) | *Cities **will be** cleaner. People **won't drive** cars.* | gelecek tahmini |
| First conditional | ***If** public transport **improves**, fewer people **will drive**.* | if + present simple → will + V1 |
| might / may | *Floods **might damage** cities.* | olasılık (emin değil) |
| can (olanak) | *Drones **can find** people under buildings.* | yetenek/olanak |
| I wish + past | *I wish everyone **had** clean air.* | şimdiki durum için dilek |
| be going to | *We **are going to use** more robots.* | planlı gelecek |

**Soru-cevap örüntüsü:** *How will self-driving cars help people?* → *They will reduce accidents because they don't get tired.* / *What will air-taxis use instead of fuel?* → *They will use batteries.* / *What will happen if we use advanced technology?* → *If we use advanced technology, we can avoid many deaths and injuries.*

### Örnek metin ve sorular (kendi örneğimiz)

> **Transport in 2050**
> Imagine a city in 2050. You get into a car, say your destination, and the car drives itself. Self-driving cars will use sensors and artificial intelligence, so there will be fewer accidents. Traffic jams will disappear because the cars will talk to each other. Above the streets, air-taxis will carry passengers to the airport in ten minutes. These flying vehicles will use batteries instead of fuel, so the air will be cleaner. Some scientists say that if we build hyperloop trains, a trip from Ankara to İstanbul will take only 30 minutes. However, there may be problems, too. Flying vehicles might be expensive, and hackers could attack the cars' systems. In my opinion, the future of transport will be fast and clean, but we must make it safe first.

| Soru | Cevap |
|---|---|
| *Why will there be fewer accidents in 2050?* | *Because self-driving cars will use sensors and artificial intelligence.* |
| *What will air-taxis use instead of fuel?* | *They will use batteries.* |
| *How long will a hyperloop trip from Ankara to İstanbul take?* | *It will take only 30 minutes.* |
| *T/F: Flying vehicles will definitely be cheap.* | *F – Flying vehicles might be expensive.* |
| *What is the writer's opinion?* | *The future of transport will be fast and clean, but it must be safe first.* |
| *Find a word that means "a person travelling in a vehicle but not driving".* | *passenger* |

## 3. Sık yapılan hatalar
- First conditional'da *if* cümlesine *will* koymak: ~~If it will rain~~ → **If it rains, we will stay at home.**
- *fried* / *fry* / *fire* karıştırmak; *boil* (haşlamak) ile *bake* (fırınlamak) karıştırmak.
- *spicy* (baharatlı/acı) ile *hot* (hem sıcak hem acı) farkı: kitapta *"Some of them can be really hot!"* = acı.
- *cuisine* (mutfak kültürü) ile *kitchen* (mutfak odası) karıştırmak.
- *Why* sorusuna *because* ile başlamamak; *What is the writer's opinion?* sorusuna kendi fikrini yazmak.
- T/F cümlesinde *definitely, never, always* gibi kesin ifadelere dikkat: metin *might* diyorsa kesin cümle **False**.
