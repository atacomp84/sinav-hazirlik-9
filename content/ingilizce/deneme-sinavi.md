**Text A (Questions 1 and 2)**

> **Green Day at Cumhuriyet High School**
> Every spring, our school celebrates Green Day. This year it was on 18 April, just before National Sovereignty and Children's Day. In the morning, the science club gave a short **lecture** about saving water, and each class had to prepare a **creative** poster. After lunch, the drama club **performed** a play about a river that lost its fish. The actors wrote the **script** themselves and practised for three weeks. Then everybody planted flowers in the schoolyard. The principal said, "You mustn't forget: protecting nature is a daily **assignment**, not a one-day event." At the end of the day, students answered a short **survey**; 90% of them said they wanted a Green Week next year.

**QUESTION 1 (ENG.8.1.R3, 14 puan, kolay)**
a) When was Green Day this year?
b) What did the science club talk about?
c) What was the play about?
d) What did the principal mean by "a daily assignment"? Explain in one sentence.
e) True or False? Correct the false one(s): 1) The students practised the play for three months. 2) Most students want a Green Week next year.

**QUESTION 2 (ENG.8.2.V1, 12 puan, kolay)**
a) Match the words in bold in Text A with the definitions. One definition is extra.
1 lecture  2 creative  3 performed  4 script  5 assignment  6 survey
i) a set of questions to collect information  ii) a piece of work you must do  iii) a formal talk to teach a subject  iv) the words of a play or film  v) acted or sang in front of people  vi) good at thinking of new ideas  vii) a formal competition with prizes
b) Complete the sentences with two of the words: *The students ________ a musical last year. / Our teacher gave us a difficult ________ about recycling.*

**Text B (Question 3)**

> My name is Derin. I live in a modern flat in Ankara with my mother, my father and my twin sister, Deniz. Our flat is comfortable but small, so Deniz and I share a room. We argue about the desk almost every day; she studies late and I want to sleep. Last month, our parents found a solution: Deniz studies in the kitchen after 10 p.m., and I tidy the room every morning. Now we get on much better. My grandparents live in a traditional wooden house in Safranbolu. It has three floors, a small courtyard and no lift. I love staying there in summer because it is quiet, and my grandfather tells me stories about the old town.

**QUESTION 3 (ENG.8.4.R3, 14 puan, orta)**
a) Why do Derin and Deniz argue?
b) How did the parents solve the problem?
c) What kind of house do Derin's grandparents live in?
d) Why does Derin love staying in Safranbolu? Give **two** reasons.
e) True / False / Not Mentioned: 1) Derin and Deniz have their own rooms. 2) The grandparents' house has a lift. 3) The grandparents are retired teachers.

**Text C (Question 5)**

> **Cars Without Drivers**
> Twenty years from now, most cars in big cities will drive themselves. Self-driving cars will use cameras and sensors to see the road, and they will communicate with each other, so there will be fewer traffic jams. They will also be electric, so the air in cities will be cleaner. Experts say the roads will be safer because computers don't get tired or check their phones. However, some people are worried. If a hacker controls the car's computer, the passengers might be in danger. Also, thousands of taxi and lorry drivers will need new jobs. In my opinion, self-driving cars will make life easier, but governments must prepare new laws before these vehicles fill our streets.

**QUESTION 5 (ENG.8.8.R3, 14 puan, orta)**
a) Why will there be fewer traffic jams?
b) Why will the air in cities be cleaner?
c) What two problems does the text mention?
d) What is the writer's opinion about self-driving cars?
e) Complete the sentence with the correct form of the verb: *If a hacker ________ (control) the car's computer, the passengers ________ (be) in danger.*
