# Cevap ve Puanlama Anahtarı — İngilizce Soru Seti

Puanlama MEB açık uçlu sorularındaki gibi: her soruda **beklenen unsurlar** listelenir, her unsur için puan verilir. Okuma cevapları anlamı taşıyorsa ve dil bilgisi hatası anlamı bozmuyorsa tam puan; kısa cevap (tek kelime) yazılmışsa o maddenin yarısı. Kelime sorularında yazım hatası (örn. *cousine*) puan düşürür. Yazma sorularında ölçüt tablosu kullanılır.

Her soru 10 puan. Toplam 200. Değerlendirme: 170+ çok iyi, 140-169 iyi (eksik unsurları tekrar et), 140 altı o kazanımın konu anlatımı yeniden okunur. Bölüm bazında yüzde hesapla; en düşük bölüm 12 Kasım'da yeniden çalışılır.

---

## BÖLÜM A — Revision 1: okul hayatı, öğrenme, dil bilgisi

**A1.** Eşleştirme 6 × 1p = 6p; iki cümle 2 × 2p (kelime doğru anlamda ve doğru türde kullanılmış).
- 1-e, 2-c, 3-b, 4-d, 5-f, 6-a; **g fazla** (survey).
- Örnek cümleler: *Our teacher gave us a difficult assignment about recycling.* / *The drama club will perform a play on Friday.*

**A2.** 1-4 her biri 2p (tam cümle 2p, doğru bilgi ama tek kelime 1p); 5. madde 2p (a için F + düzeltme 1p, b için T 1p).
1. *The science club gave a short lecture about recycling.*
2. *They planted fifty trees (in the schoolyard).*
3. *The actors made their own costumes from old clothes.*
4. *The most creative projects received awards.*
5. a) **F** – *The play was performed on Thursday evening.* b) **T**.

**A3.** 8 boşluk; 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 → 1p; 2 → 2p (iki fiil, 1p her biri). Toplam 10p.
1. performed  2. was doing / rang  3. used to  4. shouldn't  5. must  6. had to  7. will spend  8. causes

**A4.** a) Altı düzeltme × 1p = 6p. b) İki cümle × 2p = 4p (kalıp doğru 1p, mantıklı tercih 1p).
- a) *used to playing* → **used to play**; *feeled* → **felt**; *must to leave* → **must leave** (ya da *had to leave*); *he sleep* → **he sleeps**; *while he studied* → **while he was studying**; *should has* → **should have**.
- b) Örnek: *I prefer printed books to e-books because my eyes get tired on a screen.* / *I'd rather watch a video lesson than listen to a long lecture.*

## BÖLÜM B — Revision 1: aile hayatı ve ev

**B1.** 7 × 1p = 7p; hepsi doğruysa +3p (fazla kelimeyi, *spacious*, kullanmamış olmak dâhil).
1. cottage  2. nephew  3. smart home  4. get on well with  5. flat  6. cousin  7. argue

**B2.** 1-4 her biri 1,5p (6p); 5. madde 3 × 1p (3p); 6. madde 1p.
1. *He lives with his parents and his little sister.*
2. *It is in a village near İznik Lake.*
3. *He looks after the chickens and helps in the garden.*
4. *She thinks a house is not about technology; it is about the people in it.*
5. a) **F** (third floor) b) **T** c) **NM** (iş söylenmiyor; "chickens" olması çiftçi demek değil)
6. *"It" refers to a house.*

**B3.** 1-3 her biri 2p (6p); 4. madde 2 × 2p (tavsiye + gerekçe; gerekçe yoksa 1p).
1. *Her parents want her to be home by 7 p.m., but her friends stay out until 9; she and her parents argue / don't talk much.*
2. *She came home late, her father shouted and she shouted back.*
3. *Because her mother is always busy with Ela's baby brother.*
4. Örnek: *She should talk to her parents calmly and explain her feelings, because shouting makes the problem worse.* / *She shouldn't come home late without calling, because her parents worry about her.* / *She should suggest a compromise, for example 8 p.m. at weekends.* Kabul: *should/shouldn't + V1* ve tutarlı gerekçe.

## BÖLÜM C — Revision 2: dünya mutfakları ve gelecek

**C1.** 8 × 1p = 8p; tamamı doğruysa +2p. Dikkat: cümle 4 *boiled*, cümle 7 *grilled*; **fazla kelime *fried***.
1. cuisine  2. chopsticks  3. spicy  4. boiled  5. homemade  6. ingredients  7. grilled  8. dessert

**C2.** a) 4 tanım × 1p = 4p (İngilizce, anlamı veren tanım). b) 3 cümle × 2p = 6p (pişirme yöntemi, içerik, servis).
- a) *cuisine: a style of cooking from a particular place / boil: to heat water or food in very hot water until bubbles form / homemade: made at home, not bought from a shop / passenger: a person who travels in a vehicle but does not drive it.*
- b) Örnek: *Mantı is a traditional Turkish dish. It is small pieces of dough with minced meat inside, and it is boiled in water. People serve it with yoghurt, garlic and hot butter with red pepper.*

**C3.** 1-4 her biri 1,5p (6p); 5. madde 2p (a F + düzeltme 1p, b T 1p); 6. madde 2p.
1. *They can carry six passengers.*
2. *Because they will use batteries (not fuel).*
3. *They will need special landing areas on school roofs, and they might be too expensive for small towns.*
4. *The first flying school buses will start in big cities.*
5. a) **F** – *It will take only five minutes.* b) **T**.
6. *will land* (first conditional: if + present, will + V1).

**C4.** 1. madde 4 × 1p = 4p; 2-4 her biri 1,5p (4,5p); 5. madde 1,5p.
1. Advantages: *fewer accidents; old people / people who can't drive travel freely; fewer car parks / shared rides.* Disadvantages: *hackers might take control; drivers lose jobs; responsibility is unclear after an accident.* (İkişer tane yeter.)
2. *Because machines don't get tired or use phones while driving.*
3. *Self-driving cars.*
4. *We will see these cars everywhere in twenty years, but governments must write new laws first.*
5. *No. He/She thinks we will see them everywhere; the writer only wants new laws first.* Kanıt cümlesi yoksa 0,5p.

## BÖLÜM D — Theme 1: okuma ve kelime

**D1.** a) 16 boşluk × 0,5p = 8p (milliyet/dil büyük harf değilse o boşluk 0). b) 9 kelime; 3'ü yanlışsa −1, daha fazlası −2 (toplam 2p).
- a) Poland: **Polish / Warsaw**; **Hungary** / **Hungarian**; Norway: **Norwegian / Norwegian**; **Japan** / **Tokyo**; Mexico: **Mexican / Spanish**; Azerbaijan: **Azerbaijani / Baku**; **Chile / Spanish**; Kazakhstan: **Kazakh / Astana**.
- b) Countries: *Peru, Uzbekistan*. Nationalities & Languages: *Thai, Korean, Turkmen*. Attractions & activities: *palace, sightseeing, fjord, monument*.

**D2.** 1. 1p; 2. 4 × 0,75p = 3p; 3. 5 × 0,8p = 4p; 4-5 her biri 1p.
1. **b** A Journey to Kazakhstan.
2. A: location / size; B: capital and food; C: nature and activities; D: language, people and national day.
3. Kazakhstan / Astana / Kazakh / Kazakh and Russian / 16 December (Independence Day).
4. *They can see the whole city from the top.*
5. *The Charyn Canyon (is famous for its red rocks).*

**D3.** 1. 4 × 1p = 4p; 2. 1,5p; 3. 2p (çıkarım 1p + kanıt 1p); 4. 1,5p (anlam 1p + ipucu 0,5p); 5. 1p.
1. a) **F** – *Millions of people speak Quechua.* b) **T** c) **NM** d) **F** – *Visitors can't drive there; they take a train.*
2. *Because the site opens early and tickets are limited.*
3. *Lima is a good place for people who love food / Lima is a big, important city.* Kanıt: *"many people say it is the food capital of South America".*
4. *limited = there are not many / only a small number.* İpucu: *"so travellers should book weeks before"*.
5. *"Most Peruvians speak Spanish, but millions of people also speak Quechua, the language of the Incas."*

## BÖLÜM E — Theme 1: dil bilgisi

**E1.** 10 × 1p.
1. am  2. am  3. is  4. can speak  5. is  6. can visit  7. (can) take  8. are  9. can't drop  10. can see

**E2.** a) 6 × 1p = 6p (soru biçimi doğru; kelime sırası hatalıysa 0,5p). b) 3 × 1,33p ≈ 4p.
- a) 1. *What is the capital of Poland?* 2. *Where are you from?* 3. *Can she speak English / French…?* 4. *What can tourists do in Budapest / Hungary?* 5. *What is your nationality?* 6. *How many languages can she speak?*
- b) 1. ability  2. possibility  3. opportunity

**E3.** a) 6 × 1p = 6p; b) 3 × 0,5p = 1,5p; c) 2,5p (selamlama 0,5, milliyet 0,5, dil 0,5, can cümlesi 1).
- a) 1. *She **is** from Hungary…* 2. *I can **speak** Korean a little.* 3. *What **is** the capital of Peru?* 4. *He **can** swim very well.* 5. *I am from **Türkiye** / I am **Turkish**.* 6. *You can't **touch** the locals in China.*
- b) 1. *What languages can you speak?* 2. *What is Norway famous for?* 3. *What can tourists do in Tashkent?*
- c) Örnek: *Anton: Hi there! I'm Anton from Bulgaria. I'm Bulgarian and I can speak Bulgarian and English. — Karla: Nice to meet you, Anton! I'm Karla, and I'm Norwegian. I can speak Norwegian and a little German. — Anton: What can tourists do in Norway? — Karla: They can take a fjord cruise and visit Akershus Fortress.*

## BÖLÜM F — Theme 1: yazma

Ölçüt tablosu (her yazma sorusu 10p):

| Ölçüt | Puan | Tam puan koşulu |
|---|---|---|
| İçerik | 4 | Sorudaki **her istenen madde** var; kelime sınırı içinde; konu dışı yok. Eksik her madde −1. |
| Kelime | 2 | Tema kelimeleri (*capital, nationality, native language, tourist attraction, historical site, be famous for, sightseeing, national day*) doğru ve yazımı doğru; milliyet/dil büyük harfle. |
| Dil bilgisi | 2 | *to be* ve *can + V1* doğru; özne-fiil uyumu; anlamı bozan hata yok. 1-2 küçük hata −0,5; anlamı bozan hata −1. |
| Düzen | 2 | Paragrafta başlık + ana cümle + destek + sonuç; e-postada selamlama + kapanış; bağlaçlar; noktalama/büyük harf. |

**F1.** İçerik maddeleri (4p): ad + ülke/milliyet (1), diller (1), iki *can* etkinliği (1), Alina'ya soru (1). Düzen: *Hi Alina, … Best wishes,* zorunlu. Örnek (54 kelime):
> *Hi Alina, Let me introduce myself. I'm Mert and I'm from Türkiye, so I'm Turkish. My native language is Turkish, and I can speak English and a little German. In Türkiye, tourists can visit Hagia Sophia and take a boat ride on the Bosphorus. What can tourists do in Warsaw? See you soon, Mert*

**F2.** İçerik (4p): konum (0,5), başkent (0,5), dil (1), iki turistik yer/etkinlik *can* ile (1), millî gün (0,5), sonuç cümlesi (0,5). Modelin cümle yapısını izleme **Düzen** puanına girer. Örnek (66 kelime):
> **The Land of Thermal Waters** *Hungary is a country in Central Europe. Its capital is Budapest, a historical city on the Danube. Hungarians speak Hungarian, and many young people can speak English, too. Tourists can visit Buda Castle, a famous historical site, and relax in the thermal baths. In summer, they can swim and hike at Lake Balaton. On 20 August, National Day, people watch fireworks. Hungary is a perfect place for history and nature lovers.*

**F3.** İçerik (4p): başlık + konu cümlesi (0,5), yer ve tür (1), iki *can* etkinliği (1), millî gün/festival (1), sonuç (0,5). Örnek (63 kelime):
> **Sunrise on Mount Nemrut** *Mount Nemrut is one of the most fascinating tourist attractions in Türkiye. It is in Adıyaman, in the south-east, and it is a historical site with giant stone heads from the Commagene Kingdom. Visitors can climb the mountain before dawn and watch the sunrise between the statues. They can also take photos of the ancient ruins. Every October, people in the region celebrate Republic Day with parades. Nemrut is a place you can never forget.*
