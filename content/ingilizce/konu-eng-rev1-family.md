# Konu Anlatımı — Revision 1: Aile Hayatı ve Ev (Family Life & Home)

Kapsanan kazanım: **ENG.8.4.R3** — "family life and home: relationships, problems and solutions in the family; different houses and living styles" içerikli metinleri anlamlandırma.

Kaynak: MEB 9. Sınıf İngilizce Ders Kitabı, Revision 1, s. 17-18 ("A Weekend Without Phones" metninin aile ve ev bölümleri: kuzen Tom'un *smart home*'u, geleneksel ev, babanın eskiden yaşadığı *enormous old house*). Senaryo 1, 2, 3 ve 5'te **1 soru** (Senaryo 4'te yok). Dört senaryoda bulunduğu için **en sık sorulan 8. sınıf kazanımı**dır.

**Sınavda ne beklenir:** Bir aileyi, aile içi bir sorunu ve çözümünü ya da farklı ev/yaşam tarzlarını anlatan 90-130 kelimelik metin verilir. Sorular: Wh- soruları (İngilizce tam cümle), True/False/Not Mentioned, "What does *it/they/there* refer to?", "Find a word that means…", metne göre tavsiye yazma (*What should Emir do?*). Cevap metne dayanmalı; kendi fikrin sorulmadıkça ekleme yapma.

---

## 1. Metin türleri ve okuma taktiği

| Metin türü | Ne arar? | Sorulan şey |
|---|---|---|
| Aile tanıtımı | kim, kaç kişi, nerede yaşıyor, ilişkiler | *Who does Emir live with? How many people are there in the family?* |
| Sorun-çözüm metni | sorun (*problem*), neden, çözüm (*solution, advice*) | *What is the problem? What did they do to solve it? What should she do?* |
| Ev/yaşam tarzı karşılaştırması | ev türü, özellikler, avantaj-dezavantaj | *What kind of house does Tom live in? How is Emir's house different?* |
| Günlük/blog | duygu, değişim (*before → after*) | *How did Emir feel at the end of the weekend? What did he realise?* |

**Taktik:** Sorun-çözüm metninde işaret kelimelerini bul: *problem, argue, worried, but, however* → sorun; *so, decided to, should, finally, realised* → çözüm. Karşılaştırma metninde *but, while, more … than, traditional vs modern* kelimelerinin iki yanı iki farklı evi anlatır.

**Not Mentioned (NM) tuzağı:** Cümle mantıklı görünse bile metinde **yazmıyorsa** NM'dir. Örn. metin "Tom lives in a smart home" diyorsa "Tom's house is expensive" → **NM** (fiyat geçmiyor).

## 2. Kelime bilgisi: aile ve ilişkiler

| Alan | Kelimeler ve örnek |
|---|---|
| Aile üyeleri | parents, sibling, cousin (kuzen), aunt, uncle, nephew/niece, grandparents, stepfather, twin, relative (akraba) |
| İlişkiler | *get on well with* (iyi geçinmek), *look after* (bakmak), *trust, respect, support, share, spend time together, have a conversation, keep in touch* |
| Sorunlar | *argue / have an argument* (tartışmak), *feel lonely, ignore, shout, excessive phone use, lack of communication, generation gap, break a rule, come home late* |
| Çözümler | *apologise, make a compromise, set rules, talk openly, listen to each other, take a break from screens, family meeting, do chores together* |
| Duygular | *proud, worried, annoyed, relaxed, comfortable, grateful* |

**Tavsiye cümlesi kalıbı:** *He should talk to his parents instead of shouting. They shouldn't use phones at dinner. If I were her, I would apologise.*

## 3. Kelime bilgisi: ev ve yaşam tarzları

| Ev türü | Açıklama |
|---|---|
| detached house | müstakil ev; *a house that is not joined to another* |
| flat / apartment | daire; *in a block of flats, on the third floor* |
| cottage | kır evi; *a small house in the countryside* |
| villa | yazlık/lüks ev, genellikle bahçeli ve havuzlu |
| smart home | akıllı ev; *a system controls the lights, the fridge, even the bed* |
| traditional house | geleneksel ev; *old, wooden, with a courtyard, comfortable and safe* |
| enormous old house | çok büyük eski ev; *strange patterns on the walls* |
| mobile home / caravan, houseboat, treehouse, igloo, tent | farklı yaşam tarzları: hareketli, su üstünde, ağaçta, buzdan |

Özellik sıfatları: *spacious* (ferah), *cosy* (sıcak, rahat), *modern, crowded, quiet, peaceful, noisy, safe, comfortable, convenient, eco-friendly*.

**Yaşam tarzı karşılaştırması:** *Tom's family lives in a smart home in the city; everything is automatic. Emir's house is more traditional, but it is comfortable and safe. Emir's father used to live in an enormous old house before Emir was born.* Sorulursa: *How are the two houses different?* → *Tom's house is modern and controlled by a system, while Emir's house is traditional but comfortable.*

## 4. Örnek metin ve çözüm (kendi örneğimiz)

> My name is Selin. I live in a flat on the fifth floor with my parents and my twin brother, Can. Our grandparents live in a cottage near the sea, and we visit them every summer. Last month, Can and I had a big argument because he never helped with the chores. I felt annoyed and stopped talking to him. Then my mother organised a family meeting. We made a list of chores and shared them. Now Can washes the dishes and I take out the rubbish. We get on well again, and the flat is tidier than before. I think talking openly is the best solution for every family problem.

| Soru | Cevap |
|---|---|
| *Where do Selin's grandparents live?* | *They live in a cottage near the sea.* |
| *What was the problem between Selin and Can?* | *Can never helped with the chores, so they had an argument.* |
| *How did the family solve the problem?* | *Her mother organised a family meeting and they shared the chores.* |
| *T/F/NM: Can is older than Selin.* | *NM – they are twins, but the text doesn't say who is older.* |
| *What does "it" refer to in "I think talking openly is the best solution"?* | *Talking openly.* |
| *What does Selin think about family problems?* | *She thinks talking openly is the best solution.* |

## 5. Sık yapılan hatalar
- Soruda *Why* varsa cevapta **because** olmalı: *Because he never helped with the chores.*
- *How did she feel?* sorusuna sıfat yerine olay yazmak; cevap **duygu sıfatı**: *She felt annoyed.*
- Metinde geçmeyen "mantıklı" bilgiyi doğru (T) işaretlemek → **NM**.
- *live in a flat*, *live on the fifth floor*, *live with my parents* edatlarını karıştırmak.
- *cousin* ile *nephew* karıştırmak: cousin = teyze/amca çocuğu; nephew = yeğen (erkek), niece = yeğen (kız).
- Tavsiye sorusunda *must* kullanmak; tavsiye için **should**.
